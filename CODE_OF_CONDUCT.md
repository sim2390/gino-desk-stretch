# Code of conduct

Gino is a friendly little project, and we'd like everyone who takes part to feel welcome.

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was new once.
- **Talk about ideas and code, never about people.** Disagree politely and explain why.
- **No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.**
- **Assume good intent.** If something reads badly, ask before assuming.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, use **Report content** in the **···** menu on the comment, issue or pull
request.
